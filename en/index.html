Where the money goes.

  1. Subscriptions for the devs in our sponsorship programme. This comes first: every subscription is in the name of a single selected person, for as long as they work with us. No lending, no reselling.
  2. Humanizer and Entretien blanc: their AI and their hosting.
  3. Running costs: domain name, insurance, trademark filing.

Being supported means coding on our projects.

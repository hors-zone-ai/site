Questions we get asked

Is it really free?

Yes. You pay nothing, not when you apply and not later.

Who can apply?

Devs and computer science students who can't afford these tools.

What do I do in return?

Code with us on the association's apps, for as long as you have access. When the work stops, the access goes to someone else.

Are you linked to Anthropic or OpenAI?

No. We pay for official plans, like any other customer. The brands mentioned belong to their owners.

What happens to my data?

It's used to review your application, nothing else. We keep it while we review it and, if you join the programme, up to 12 months after it ends. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

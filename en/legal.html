What you give us: for an application to the sponsorship programme, your first and last name, your email, your GitHub, the tool you chose and your motivation letter. For an organisation: its email and its message.

Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Where: the answers go to n8n, an automation tool hosted in the European Union, which processes them on behalf of the association.

How long: as long as it takes to review your application or request and, if you join the programme, until the end of your participation, then 12 months at most. You can ask for deletion at any time.

Your rights: access, correction, deletion, objection. Write to us through that form or reply to any email from us. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
